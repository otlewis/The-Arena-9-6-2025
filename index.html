📖 Parents' Guide to The Arena DTD

Welcome!
Your teen wants to join The Arena DTD, a real-time debate and discussion app where young people sharpen their critical thinking and communication skills. This guide explains what you need to know as a parent or guardian.

👥 Who can use Arena?

  • 13–17 years old: Can join with your approval at $4.99/month.
  • 18+: Can subscribe directly at $9.99/month.
  • Under 13: Not allowed on Arena.

💳 How do subscriptions work?

  • Subscriptions are billed securely through the App Store or Google Play.
  • Teens can't activate a subscription without your consent.
  • Subscriptions renew monthly or yearly unless canceled.
  • You can pause or cancel anytime.

💎 What are Arena Coins?

Arena Coins are our virtual currency.

Listeners can buy Coins to:

  • Gift debaters in live rooms 🎁
  • Vote in debates 🗳️
  • Unlock special features 🎖️

Coins have no real-world cash value and can't be exchanged for money.

🔒 What information do you collect?

  • For your teen: Username, email, date of birth, and activity inside the app.
  • For you (parent/guardian): Just your email for consent and account controls.
  • Payment info: Handled by Apple/Google — we don't store card details.
We do not sell data.

🛡️ What control do I have as a parent?

You can at any time:

  • ✅ Approve or decline your teen's subscription.
  • ⏸ Pause or cancel the subscription.
  • 🗑 Request deletion of your teen's account and data.
  • 📂 Ask for a copy of what information we hold.

📩 How do I contact Arena?

If you ever have questions or want to manage your teen's account, email us at:
thearenadtd@gmail.com

⚖️ Safety & Compliance

  • We follow COPPA (U.S.) and GDPR-K (Europe) rules for teen data.
  • Teens under 13 cannot use Arena.
  • Parental consent is always required for 13–17-year-olds.

✅ In short:

Arena is free to listen, safe to use, and requires your approval for your teen to participate. You stay in control at every step.
